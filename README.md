# NorthStar Fintech Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes customer interactions, product performance, transaction outcomes, revenue, retention, errors, and customer satisfaction for **NorthStar Fintech UK Ltd.** The analysis identifies product performance patterns, operational challenges, and opportunities to improve customer experience and business performance.

## 2. Project Objectives

* Evaluate product and transaction performance.
* Identify factors affecting transaction success, errors, and customer satisfaction.
* Analyze revenue and retention across products and customer segments.
* Identify operational areas requiring improvement.
* Develop data-driven recommendations to improve product performance and customer experience.

## 3. Key Findings

* **Instant Bank Transfer** recorded 18% of interactions, with an **82% completion rate** and **77.8% success rate**, while errors accounted for **13.7%**.
* **Direct Debit Manager** recorded an **80.2% success rate**, **9.9% error rate**, and **72.16% customer retention**.
* **International Transfer** generated the highest revenue per transaction at approximately **€169.54**.
* **Premium customers** recorded a **67.75% retention rate**.
* **Budget Planner** generated negative revenue, indicating an area requiring further review.
* Customer satisfaction and retention varied across products, highlighting opportunities for targeted product improvements.

## 4. Recommendations

* **Improve transaction reliability** by investigating error sources and reducing failure points in transaction processes.
* **Strengthen customer retention** by improving engagement and service quality, particularly for products and customer segments with lower retention.
* **Review underperforming products** such as Budget Planner to identify the causes of negative revenue and assess opportunities for improvement.
* **Leverage high-performing products** by identifying successful practices from products with stronger transaction success, retention, and revenue.
* **Improve customer experience** by using satisfaction and transaction data to identify and address recurring customer pain points.

## 5. Tools Used

* **Microsoft Excel** — data cleaning, analysis, calculations, and dashboard development.
* **Power Pivot** — data modeling, measures, and analysis of customer and product performance.
* **PivotTables & PivotCharts** — performance analysis and visualization.
* **Data Analysis** — revenue, success rates, errors, retention, satisfaction, and product performance analysis.
